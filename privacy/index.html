Privacy Policy

Beaver Blast (package name: com.vagongames.beaverblast) · Effective date: 10 November 2026

This policy explains what data the mobile game Beaver Blast ("the game") collects, how it is used and with whom it is shared. The game is published by Abdulkerim Yıldırım, Vagon Games ("we"), based in Türkiye, who is the data controller.

In short
  • The game has no accounts. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your device.
  • The game shows ads (Google AdMob), sells optional in-app purchases (processed by Google Play or the App Store, managed through RevenueCat) and uses Google Firebase for usage statistics and crash reports. These services receive device identifiers and usage data as described below.

1. Data we collect and why

Data stored only on your device

Your progress (levels, coins, gems, unlocked characters, settings and language) is saved in an encrypted file on your device. To detect a save file copied from another device, that file contains your device's identifier. This file is not uploaded to us or anyone else, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Data collected through third-party services

2. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with the service providers that the game needs in order to work, each of which processes it under its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform (advertising and consent) and Google Firebase (analytics and crash reporting) — Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited: Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses data from apps that use its services. Ads may be supplied by Google's advertising partners, who receive the advertising data described above.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. (purchase management): RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play or the Apple App Store (payments), under the terms you accepted with your store account.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it, or if the game is transferred to another owner, in which case this policy continues to apply to data already collected.

3. Consent and your choices

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: on first launch the game shows a consent form. Personalised ads are shown only with your consent. Analytics is switched on only if you consent to storing and accessing information on your device. You can change your answer at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy options.
  • Other regions: analytics is on by default. You can limit ad personalisation in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking), where you can also reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • iOS: the game asks for your permission before tracking you across other companies' apps and websites.
  • Crash reports are sent in every region, based on our legitimate interest in keeping the game stable.
  • A one-time Remove Ads purchase turns off interstitial ads. Rewarded ads are always optional.

4. Legal bases

Where the GDPR, UK GDPR or the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK, Law No. 6698) applies, we rely on: your consent (personalised advertising, and analytics in the regions listed above), performance of a contract (delivering and restoring purchases), and our legitimate interests (crash reporting, fraud and cheat prevention, non-personalised advertising, and analytics outside the regions listed above).

5. Retention

  • Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • Analytics data linked to an identifier is kept by Google for up to 14 months; statistics that no longer identify a device may be kept longer.
  • Crash reports are kept for 90 days.
  • Purchase records are kept for as long as needed to restore your purchases and to meet legal and accounting obligations.
  • Advertising data is kept by Google according to its own retention policies.

6. International transfers

Our service providers may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. They use safeguards such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses where required.

7. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the game, contact us and we will take steps to have it deleted.

8. Your rights and data deletion

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, to withdraw consent at any time and to complain to your data protection authority.

  • To delete the data stored on your device, uninstall the game or clear its data in your device settings.
  • To stop further collection, uninstall the game. Resetting or deleting your advertising ID breaks the link between your device and past advertising data.
  • To request deletion of analytics, crash or purchase data held by our service providers, email us at vagonsoft@gmail.com. Because the game has no accounts, we may ask you for the identifiers needed to find your data. We answer within 30 days.

9. Security

Data sent by the game is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS), and the save file on your device is encrypted. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or the law changes. The current version is always on this page, with its effective date at the top. Significant changes will also be announced in the game or on its store page.
